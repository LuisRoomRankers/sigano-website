The remit

One team handling every vendor.

The same instinct that shapes the music shapes the room. Sigano coordinates and manages full event production — lighting, photography, videography, and editing — through trusted specialists, so you brief one person and the whole evening comes together to a single, consistent standard.

i.

Lighting

Ambient uplighting that colors the room to your palette and responds to the music, moving-head dance-floor lights for energy when the night opens up, and premium effects — cold sparks, or a low-lying "dancing on a cloud" fog — through trusted specialists, subject to venue approval.

ii.

Photography

Event and performance photography coverage, shot to match the tone of the evening — candid moments, the room as it was designed, and the people in it. Sourced and directed alongside the rest of production so nothing is missed.

iii.

Videography

Cinematic event coverage, plus the content capture Sigano already delivers as standard — beautifully shot highlight clips from the night, so the energy of the evening is preserved the way it actually felt.

iv.

Editing

Edited highlight reels and short social clips delivered afterward — ready to keep, to share, and to remember the night by. Cut to the music, finished to the same standard as everything else.

Production FAQ

What clients ask about production.

Can I book production without the music?
Yes. While most clients bring Sigano in for the full evening, lighting, photography, videography, and editing can be coordinated as standalone production services. Tell us what you need in the inquiry and we'll build the proposal around it.
Do you actually shoot, or do you coordinate other vendors?
Both, depending on the service. Sigano delivers content capture and highlight editing directly, and sources and manages trusted photography, videography, and lighting specialists for fuller coverage — directed and coordinated by Sigano as a single point of contact, so the styles stay consistent.
Are premium effects like cold sparks always available?
They depend on your venue's fire and safety approvals. Cold sparks and low-lying "dancing on a cloud" fog are arranged through trusted specialists and confirmed once the date and location are set, so we know exactly what your venue allows.
When do we receive the edited photos and video?
Highlight clips and short social reels are turned around shortly after the event so you can share the night while it's fresh. Full galleries and longer cinematic edits follow on the timeline agreed in your proposal.
