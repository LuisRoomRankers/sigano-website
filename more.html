Private guitar lessons · Berklee-trained

Study flamenco & the cigano-gitano tradition at the source.

Sergio teaches advanced flamenco and the cigano-style playing rooted in the gitano tradition — the rhythmic, expressive language he plays on Miami's most demanding stages every weekend. A Berklee College of Music graduate, mentored in the tradition by Paco Aguilera, he teaches the real craft, not a textbook copy of it.

Who it's for: intermediate to advanced players who already have the fundamentals and want to go deeper — flamenco rhythm and falsetas, cigano-gitano phrasing, Spanish repertoire, and contemporary technique. Lessons are one-on-one, in person in the Miami area or online over Zoom, offered as single sessions or as multi-lesson packages for committed students.

Every student starts with a diagnostic first lesson — where you are, where you want to be, what you have time to practice — and the curriculum is built around what you want to play, not a generic syllabus.
