The founder

The artist who curates it all — Sergio de Gouveia.

Sergio de Gouveia is the founder of Sigano Entertainment and its lead performer — a Berklee-trained guitarist and bilingual DJ who built the company to curate and manage entire events, not just play them. Mediterranean flair, world-class musicianship, and a producer's eye for the whole evening.

A graduate of the world-renowned Berklee College of Music, he has been performing for over eight years at luxury venues including the Four Seasons, Fontainebleau, and The Ritz-Carlton, as well as for distinguished private clients and celebrities.

Blending elegance with versatility, Sergio offers a wide-ranging repertoire suitable for all ages and occasions — from cocktail hours and weddings to dinners and private parties. With every piece memorized and the ability to perform as a roaming guitarist, he creates an unforgettable, interactive musical experience. By combining his skills as both guitarist and DJ, Sergio delivers a distinctive performance that is as innovative as it is elegant.

Whether you're hosting an intimate dinner or a grand celebration, Sergio is the perfect choice to add a sophisticated touch to your event.

Frequently asked

The questions every planner asks first.

How far in advance should we book Sergio?
Luxury Miami weddings typically book Sigano Entertainment 9–12 months out. Peak Saturdays in October, November, March, and April reserve earliest. Use Check My Date at the top of the page to see exact availability for your wedding date.
Do you travel outside Miami?
Yes — Sigano Entertainment serves Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties with no travel fees. Destination weddings in the Florida Keys, the Bahamas, and farther are quoted on request and include flight, lodging, and gear shipping in the proposal so there are no surprises.
What's included in your wedding packages?
Every wedding package includes ceremony guitar, cocktail-hour set, professional PA system, wireless lapel mic for the officiant, fully insured performance, and a 60-minute planning consultation. DJ services for the reception are an optional add-on (Tier II and Tier III packages).
Can you play specific songs we request?
Absolutely. Sergio prepares custom arrangements for your processional, recessional, and first dance at no extra charge. Most couples submit 3–5 requests; the rest of the repertoire is curated to match your event's energy and the venue's acoustics.
Can you provide a full band, or photography, lighting, and sound?
Yes — that's the heart of what Sigano Entertainment does. Beyond solo guitar and DJ, we recruit and manage full ensembles (duos, trios, salsa bands, flamenco groups, and showbands) and coordinate event production like photography, videography, lighting, and sound — so every performer and vendor on your event runs through one point of contact.
Are you bilingual?
Yes — Sergio is fully bilingual (Spanish & English). He MCs ceremonies in either language or both, and his repertoire spans classical Spanish guitar, flamenco, Latin pop, and Mediterranean standards alongside American pop, jazz, and the modern wedding canon.
What kind of events do you NOT play?
Sergio focuses on weddings, cocktail hours, corporate events, and high-end private dinners. He does not play nightclubs, bar mitzvahs without an officiant component, or events below our event minimum.

Is your date open?

Check Sergio's calendar in under 30 seconds. We respond within 24 hours with availability and a custom proposal for your venue.
