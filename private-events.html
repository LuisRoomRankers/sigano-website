Private-event FAQ

What hosts and planners ask before they book.

Can you do just the cocktail hour, or the whole evening?
Both. Many clients book a standalone live-guitar set for a cocktail hour or reception; others want Sergio to carry the full evening — live guitar to set the mood, then a bilingual DJ for the dance floor. One artist, one timeline, one invoice. We shape the format on a quick planning call.
Can you issue a Certificate of Insurance for our venue?
Yes — Sigano Entertainment holds $2M general liability and can issue COIs naming any venue, host, or production company as additionally insured within 24 hours of a signed contract. Sergio carries the policy year-round, so there's never a "renewal in progress" surprise.
Do you sign NDAs and keep events private?
Routinely. Sergio signs client-side NDAs for brand activations, private celebrity events, and sensitive functions before any deal-specific details are shared. Default behavior is total discretion — no social posts, no event tagging, no name-drops.
Can you coordinate with our in-house or venue AV team?
Yes. Sergio brings his own self-contained, battery-powered rig but can also patch into venue PAs or production AV consoles. He calls AV leads the week before to align on power, monitor mix, and load-in. Most AV directors say his rider is the cleanest they receive.
Do you take song requests for celebrations?
Always. For birthdays, anniversaries, and holiday parties, Sergio prepares the songs that matter to you and entertains requests on the night. He works from a huge pool of music across genres and reads the room — quiet and ambient for dinner, then up for the dance floor when it's time.
