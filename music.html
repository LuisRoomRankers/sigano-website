Three ways to hear the night

Live guitar. A DJ. A band.

Most events move through all three — strings for the arrival, a DJ for the dance floor, and an ensemble when the moment deserves it. Choose one, or let the evening flow from one into the next, all coordinated by a single artist.

i.

Live Spanish Guitar

Live Mediterranean guitar shaped to your mood — flamenco, classical, Latin, and tasteful reinterpretations of the songs you love. Ideal for the ceremony and cocktail hour, played seated on a stage or roaming among your guests, table to table.

ii.

DJ Sets

Bilingual DJing that reads the room in real time — Latin, Top-40, Mediterranean, and house, mixed seamlessly and never on autopilot. The transition from live guitar to the DJ rig is invisible, so the energy of the reception only ever builds.

iii.

Curated Ensembles

When one guitar isn't enough: duos and trios, a full salsa band, a flamenco group, or a curated showband — assembled and led by Sergio from a roster of trusted South Florida musicians, scaled to your space and your guests.

The repertoire

A songbook that travels.

The set is never generic. From the first note of the ceremony to the last song of the night, the music is chosen for your guests — the room Sergio is reading, and the cultures in it.

Live-guitar favorites range from the cinematic — La Vie en Rose, O Sole Mio, Alabina — through flamenco, rumba, bossa nova, and Latin standards, alongside Spanish-guitar reinterpretations of the modern songs couples request.

And it's genuinely multilingual. Sergio performs and MCs across English, Spanish, Portuguese, and French, with songs in Italian, Arabic, and beyond — the reason cross-cultural weddings, embassy galas, and international guest lists feel at home from the first chord.

About the music

What clients ask about the repertoire.

What does the live guitar repertoire include?
A Mediterranean core — flamenco, rumba, classical, bossa nova, and Latin standards — alongside cinematic favorites like La Vie en Rose, O Sole Mio, and Alabina. Sergio also prepares Spanish-guitar arrangements of modern songs, so the set always feels chosen for your event rather than pulled from a stock list.
What languages can you perform and MC in?
English, Spanish, Portuguese, and French fluently, with songs in Italian, Arabic, and other languages on request. It's why cross-cultural weddings and international guest lists are among our most-booked events — the music and the announcements honor every tradition in the room.
Is the DJ the same person as the guitarist?
Yes. Sergio plays the live guitar and runs the DJ set himself, so the handoff from cocktail-hour strings to the reception dance floor is one continuous experience — no second vendor, no second invoice, no awkward transition between acts.
Can guests request songs during the event?
Yes — with judgment. Sergio honors any request that fits the room's current energy and your pre-approved do-not-play list. For the live-guitar sets, sharing a few favorites ahead of time lets him prepare custom arrangements you'll actually have heard before the day.
How large can the ensembles be?
From an intimate duo or trio up to a full salsa band, flamenco group, or curated showband. Sergio assembles and leads the ensemble from a roster of trusted South Florida musicians, scaled to your space, your headcount, and the sound you have in mind. Tell us the vision on your planning call.
