Three ways to hear the night

Live guitar. A DJ. A band.

Strings for the arrival, a DJ for the dance floor, an ensemble when the moment deserves it — choose one, or let the night flow through all three.

i.

Live Spanish Guitar

Live Mediterranean guitar shaped to your mood — flamenco, classical, Latin. Seated on stage or roaming table to table, ideal for the ceremony and cocktail hour.

ii.

DJ Sets

Bilingual DJing that reads the room in real time — Latin, Top-40, Mediterranean, and house. The move from live guitar to the DJ rig is invisible.

iii.

Curated Ensembles

When one guitar isn't enough — duos, a salsa band, a flamenco group, or a showband, assembled and led by Sergio.

The repertoire

A songbook that travels.

The set is never generic — chosen for your guests, the room, and the cultures in it.

From La Vie en Rose and O Sole Mio through flamenco, rumba, and bossa nova — to Spanish-guitar takes on the modern songs couples request.

And genuinely multilingual — Sergio performs and MCs in English, Spanish, Portuguese, and French, with songs in Italian, Arabic, and beyond.
